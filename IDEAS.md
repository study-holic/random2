# Idea list (seed v0 from the cloud session; the lead extends it to at least 25 and runs /evaluate on it)

Each idea adds behaviour. Rules references are GameRules.md section names. Cards from scout-web land in ideas/cross-domain.md.

| # | Idea | Mechanism (rule) | Maps | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tribute interrupt | Stun, death, or a new altar lock cancels a tribute ("Obtaining Resources", "Channels"). A 100-gem bag channels 10 turns. Our stunners (Ryth, Stellar, Marcella) target a tributing enemy first, and one champion carries a 10-gold lock and places it when an enemy starts a channel of 3 or more ticks within range | All | `turn` pre phase plus `buy` |
| 2 | Carrier hunting | Carried gems pass to the killer on death ("Carried gems can be stolen on death"). A kill on a full carrier is a double swing. One fast champion (Vendetta, Vesper Vale) shadows the enemy route between their camps and altar and attacks carriers with 30+ gems when the fight evaluator says it wins | Contact maps: rows, lanes, rushbait, collision | `turn` mid phase |
| 3 | Capture denial and steal | An enemy on a capture region resets the 5-round timer ("Camps & capture"). After the enemy clears an Elk, one champion steps onto the region; if their side leaves or dies, we capture the rewards they paid health for | Elk maps: elkring, eight, stampede, ambush | `turn` mid, `target` |
| 4 | Contest objective control | Contest has 2 monoliths (200 gems, 300 gold, 150-round respawn) and 12 gold groups (20 gold camps), with no gem camps. Panel bots finish on 0 gems, so one paid capture wins the map. Measured: the monolith keeps its damage between visits, and the opponent took the capture in all 4 chip games (notes/contest-experiments.md). Next: vision first, chip only with no enemy in 8 tiles, burst the last 1,500, hold the capture with every healthy champion | contest | `map_contest.cpp` (draft in candidates/) |
| 5 | Counter-draft | `draft::current_draft(team::enemy)` shows the enemy's picks during the draft. Answer dive comps (Vendetta, Varg) with stuns and answer farm comps with hunters | All | `draft` |
| 6 | Endgame all-in | When the enemy's tributed gems pass about 850, every champion goes to their altars to interrupt and lock; when ours pass 850, carriers tribute in small bags | All | `turn` pre |
| 7 | Lock our own altar | A lock blocks opposing tributes and lets its owner tribute ("Altar locks"). On maps with 2 altars, our carrier places a lock on the shared altar before tributing a big bag | contest, maps with central altars | `turn` mid |
| 8 | Bush ambush | Hidden champions in bushes block movement and are unseen ("Hidden enemies still block"). Wait in a bush beside the enemy's route to their altar and hit carriers | Maps with bushes (contest has 26) | `turn` mid |
| 9 | Teleport-crystal rotations | A crystal moves 3 tiles over terrain after a 3-turn channel that damage does not stop ("Consumables") | Large maps: ambush, stampede, pilgrimage | `buy`, `turn` |
| 10 | Willow heal composition (retest) | Willow heals an ally or resets its attack. The heal sustains a tank on an Elk or the monolith. The GitHub copy holds no Willow test and no bot casts her heal (mining/retest.md), so the rejection Xans remembers sits in the later local work; retest on every map where ladder data is neutral or positive (avoid ambush and corners, where its z is -3.6 and -3.4) | contest, elkring, stampede | `draft`, `turn` |
| 11 | Per-map ladder drafts | Best draft per map from META-current-layouts.md (for example ambush C&I, Ren, Ryth, Stellar, Varg at z +3.6; rushbait C&I, Ryth, Varg, Vendetta at z +3.4); drop Vendetta on eight, contest, and stampede (z -5.8, -3.5, -3.1) | All | `draft` |
| 12 | Gold tiebreak play | Games that reach round 2,000 compare tributed gems, then lifetime gold. Contest reaches round 2,000 in 69 per cent of ladder games, stampede 10, rushbait 6. Late in a level game, farm gold camps | contest, stampede, rushbait | `target` |
| 13 | Swirl and rushbait route rebuild | v4.14 needs 1,545 rounds on swirl against foundry-v16's 785. Read how foundry-v16 routes on these maps, then write our own version in the map files | swirl, rushbait | map files |
| 14 | Fight evaluator | Compare rounds-to-kill both ways from visible health and damage; engage on a margin, else regroup. Applies to the packs losses against foundry-v16 | Contact maps | `turn` pre |
