## 3. Model (P_S1_AUCTION; s1econ.cpp)
Source domain: multi-robot task allocation with time windows (operations research and robotics). Sequential single-item auctions: Koenig et al. 2006, AAAI; time windows: Nunes and Gini 2015, AAAI (TeSSI). Champions are robots, camp captures and tributes are tasks, the shared-memory claim bytes are the bid board.

- **Objective:** maximise tributed gems per round for the team.
- **Value rate** of champion i for capture task j:
  u_ij = [ (G_j(1 − ρ) + λ O_j) / k_j − ρ (C_i + B_i) ] / ( w_τ τ_ij + w_e max(0, e_j − t − τ_ij) + s_j )
  - G_j, O_j: `camp_group::gem_value/gold_value`; λ = 0.1 gem per gold (P_S1_LAMBDA).
  - τ_ij: walking rounds, from one distance table per group built after init (engine `distance_field::compute`, copied once into wasm memory, at most one per turn while 70k fuel remain).
  - e_j: respawn round from vision (`camp_group::state`) or capture round + respawn; 0 = believed alive.
  - s_j = HP_j ÷ (k_j × dps_i) + 5 capture rounds + the group's span (max side of its spawn-tile box); dps_i = 1.5 × strength.
  - ρ = 1 − (1 − ρ_camp)(1 − ρ_enemy). ρ_camp = clamp((r − R)/R, 0, 1), with r = Σstrength_j × clear time ÷ (k_j × max HP_i) and R = 1.0: the damage the coalition takes against its pooled health (monsters hit the nearest champion, arena test 2). ρ_enemy = 0.25 per enemy seen within 6 tiles of the group in the last 40 rounds (cap 0.8).
  - C_i = 60 × income per champion-round + λ × 50 gold, with income = max(banked ÷ (champions × round), map prior Σ_j G_j/(respawn_j + 5) ÷ (2 × champions)); B_i = carried gems.
  - Mode 1 (pure auction) uses k_j = the smallest coalition with r ≤ R; mode 2 (hybrid, delivered) uses k_j = 1 for solo groups and, for squad groups, the squad members alive (non-zero claim bytes), because v4's squads are the only mechanism that actually brings a coalition together.
- **Constraints:** one claimant per solo group; a claimed solo group is taken only by an ETA bucket at least 2 better (outbid); squad groups through v4's squad claim; one capturer (capture_rank).
- **Decision rule (mode 2):** v4's choose_target passes (contested skip, own half first, squad claim), with the score replaced by −100 u_ij; u ≤ 0 is a last resort (score 1000 + distance). u is computed for the 10 candidates with the best d + 2·wait by bounding-box distance; the rest rank after them. Mode 1: best u over 8 candidates every turn, hysteresis u_new > 1.2 u_cur, outbid rule, wait outside a heavy group until its coalition arrives (P_S1_ASSEMBLE).
- **Claim byte** (bot.cpp:667-672): bits 0-5 = group + 1, bits 6-7 = ETA bucket (≤ 10, ≤ 25, ≤ 50, > 50 rounds). Every reader masks with 0x3F (cluster.cpp:67,119; micro.cpp:97; siege.cpp:277; squads.cpp:140,209).
- **Deviation from the brief, measured:** the pure auction (mode 1) has no way to form a coalition: each champion values a heavy group as if k allies will join, nobody joins, and the lone bidder retreats and returns. With assembling on it was slower than v4 unopposed (median +17 %, corridors 253 → 22 gems); without, the median was −6 % but corridors and ambush collapsed (+708 %, +60 %). The hybrid keeps v4's squads as the coalition and puts u_ij on every choice.

## 6. Fuel
- Gate (vs starter-cpp): max turn 97,209 (v4: 90,697; the switches-off copy g0: 92,042). PASS, but above the 80,000 guideline. The S1 code itself costs: target choice about 20k on lanes (v4's own about 16k), distance tables 4-13k each (built only with 70k spare, one a turn), threat detour one extra path field (20-35k on big maps, only when the remaining fuel covers it twice).
- Measured fuel facts (FUELDBG stamps): a path field costs about 30k on fracture (61 × 71); v4's update_contested cost 32k with six enemies in view; a 42-iteration loop over groups costs 7-10k (fuel is charged per instruction, so short loops are not free); `distance_field::get` is not the bottleneck.
- Against v4 at salt 0, 2 turns in 42 games still ran out of fuel (boss_battle, lanes); salted copies more (their first-turn burn). The governors stop optional work; they do not cap v4's own code.

## 7. For integration
- **Byte map (correction to the brief):** v4 writes the squad claims to bytes 6 and 7 (`write_squad_claim`, kSquadClaimByte 6 + squad) on every map with squads, not only rows or siege; and with Cassi and Issac a team has 6 bodies, so champion 5 writes byte 5. S1 keeps bytes 0-5 as claims (task | ETA bucket) and v4's squad claims in 6-7. Byte 5 for S3's focus target and byte 6 for a channel alert would collide; S3 needs a different layout (for example the ETA bits are free to drop if bytes are scarce).
- All changes are behind `P_S1_*` in `bots/s1/src/a`: new files `s1.h`, `s1econ.cpp`; edits in bot.cpp, bot.h, squads.cpp, micro.cpp, util.cpp, util.h, stuck.cpp, cluster.cpp, siege.cpp (claim masks). With every P_S1 switch at 0 the bot is v4 plus the measurement salt (and the same fuel-neutral claim masks).
- To build v4 + the two safe fixes only: `python3 tools/s1/variant.py <name> P_S1_AUCTION=0 P_S1_THREAT_COST=0 P_S1_ONE_CAPTURER=0` (= g1: gate PASS max 93,267; 74/126 vs v4; unopposed s1b row).
