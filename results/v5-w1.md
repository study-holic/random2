## 2. Camp threat model

**Why range 4 hurt in v4's ablation.** `incoming_damage` and `danger_zone` (util.cpp) count every LIVE camp at range + 1, asleep or awake. With elk at 4 every champion within 5 of a sleeping elk adds its 60 to the retreat test (`need = incoming x 4 + 10 %`), so champions near elk groups retreat or detour without being hit. The measured rules say asleep camps never attack, and an awake camp hits only the nearest champion.

**Measured in new arena tests** (bots/arena-probe scenarios 14-16, maps-arena/p14-p16):
- A Monolith does **not regenerate**: hit to 19,950, watched from distance 8 for 380 rounds, unchanged. Champions do not heal away from spawn either.
- "Nearest" is **Chebyshev**. With A diagonal at Chebyshev 3 (Euclid² 18) and B straight at Chebyshev 4 (Euclid² 16), the elk hit A every round and B never. With A straight at 3 and B diagonal at 3, it alternated every round.

**Built: `P_THREAT_MODEL`** (util.h:40-56, util.cpp:44-100): only awake camps count, at the API range of their kind + 1, and only when no other visible champion is strictly nearer (Chebyshev; a tie counts). Danger zone = radius min(range + 1, nearest other champion's distance). Awake camps and champion tiles are read once a round.
**Built: `P_VIEW_RANGE` 1** (util.h:47-49, bot.cpp:78-90): Monolith groups enter the turn view at 6 tiles instead of 4. Trace (contest vs v3b-e1, round 176-179): a Cassi walked from 7 to 4 tiles of an awake Monolith, alone, losing 200 a hit with `incoming` 0, because `build_view` kept non-target camps only within 4 tiles (the Monolith hits at 5). Elk groups stay at 4: horizon 5 for elk cost about 7 wins vs v4.

The threat model is neutral to slightly negative in self-play in the final configuration: it adds no wins and does not cut monster deaths (4.1 a game either way). It is shipped **OFF** (`P_THREAT_MODEL 0`); the code stays for a real-team test. The brief's "never be the nearest champion to an awake camp unless finishing it" and "wake a camp only when the squad can kill it" are not implemented beyond this model.

## 4. Contest (brief addendum)

**Real-team traces** (data/real/v4-panel micron-A and deerpark-A, map 2):
- Camps: 32 gold clusters and 2 Monoliths. The only gem sources are the Monoliths (200 each) and champion kills.
- Our deaths: micron 54, deerpark 51, **all to enemy champions** (51 of 54 and 51 of 51 by the method above). We carried no gems when we died.
- Opponents' gems come from the Monoliths. Micron's team did 98,900 damage to them (Varg 31k, Ren 28k, Vendetta 21k, Ryth 15k, rounds 1,162-1,860) and reached 1,000 gems. Deerpark's Ryth alone did 46,929 over 319 hit-rounds with **5,400-5,900 max HP** (health shards), losing about 180 a round and healing at spawn between visits.
- v4 farms gold clusters all game (siege off) and scores 0.

**Built: `P_CONTEST_SIEGE2`** (map_contest.cpp:44-205), shipped OFF. It runs team sessions on the Monolith nearer our spawn: gather 7 tiles out, melee adjacent and ranged at 3. A champion leaves while it has the health for the hits on the way out (it is the nearest), and a session aborts when an enemy is within 6. Melee champions buy health shards on contest, and melee join at 2,500+ max HP. Also `set_mono_zone_skip` (util.cpp:173, util.h:84).
