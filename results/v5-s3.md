## 2. Contest

### Cause trace (before building)
- **Real games** (9 top-10 games, set A). v4 does 0 Monolith damage in 7 of 9 games and dies 31-82 times. The top teams start on a Monolith at r217-775, bank 200 at a time from the central altars, and repeat about every 95 rounds. During their 38 channels of 200 gems, the escort within 5 of the carrier had a median of 1, and v4's nearest live champion was 4-25 tiles away. An interdiction target exists.
- **Self-play** (v4 vs v3b-e1 and vs v4): 0 gems (v4 tributes 0 in 10 of 10 mirror games). Old siege code (`siege.cpp`) is off since engine 1.0 raised the Monolith's range to 5.
- **Monolith aggro, measured on s3 replays** (`/tmp` scripts aggro2/aggro3, 6,900 hits): one hit of 200 a round on the champion nearest after the round's moves (Chebyshev, 100 % of hits against the end-of-round positions), ties to the lower champion index (2,399 of 2,556). It never attacks while asleep. The site doc's "range 3" and the arena note "ties alternate" do not match engine 1.0 here.
- The first build lost about 100 champions a game. The traced causes, each fixed in turn: walking in while it was awake (the first champion in range is nearest for 4-5 steps); leaving at distance 5 with a recall that every hit interrupts; v4's unseen-hit rule walking a hurt champion home through the range; v4's stuck detector sending a standing tank home every other turn; 800-health Vendetta taking a distance-1 slot; v4's bank giving up on contested altars, so a 200-gem carrier stood 12+ rounds beside an altar or walked off to farm.

### Model and rules (s3_contest.cpp header holds the full statement)
- **P_S3_MONO (own Monolith).** Objective: bank 200-gem Monoliths without feeding. Constraint: the nearest champion takes 200 a round. Decision rule: approach while it sleeps, then wake it with 3 of us within 3. Melee stand at 1 (max health >= 950 only) and ranged at 3, their attack range; they move or attack, not both (GameRules l.50). Leave at health < 400 while a healthy ally is as near (one step back hands over the aggro), else at < 200 x (6 - d) + 200: a safety stock of the hits needed to get out (inventory theory). Leave is sticky until healed to 700. The healthiest visible ally captures. Source: tank rotation on a raid boss (Roshan, Baron).
- **P_S3_CARRY.** Decision rule: a bag of >= 100 goes to the central altar with fewer visible enemies within 3 and submits from distance 1. Damage does not interrupt a tribute, and a submit against a lock becomes an attack on the lock (GameRules l.181, l.199).
- **P_S3_INTERDICT (play a).** A carrier with bag g channels T = ceil(g/10) turns. Decision rule: a lock-holder within 2 of that altar places its lock (cancels the channel); else a ready stun on the carrier; else walk in when dist <= 16, which arrives inside the channel when dist - 2 < T. Sightings are shared in byte 7 (contest has no squad groups, so bytes 6-7 are free, as w9's siege used them). Each champion buys a lock at the shop. Source: MOBA objective denial (contest the channel, not the fight). Traced in s3 contest games: on contest__v3b-e1__B__s4, our Varg's lock at r1835 cancelled their C&I's 200-gem channel (started r1823). They restarted at r1841, and our C&I's lock at r1849 cancelled it again. The bag stayed unbanked.
- **P_S3_ESCORT** (champions without a bag walk beside a carrier of >= 100): off after its ablation.
