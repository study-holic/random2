## 2. Contest module (s3_contest.cpp)

### 2.1 Model

Source domains: operations research (tank rotation as a cyclic schedule; staging as a rendezvous before a synchronised start), MOBA play (the Baron/Roshan pattern: the team tanks an objective with one front-liner in aggro and rotates, and the carrier is escorted), and the top-10 replays themselves (Pantheon, Sponge, procless on contest; `v4top-t0{2,5,6}-A` game 2).

Terms mapped to rules:
- Monolith M: 20,000 health, no regeneration, 200 damage a round to the **nearest** champion within range 5, keeps its target until someone is nearer, never attacks while asleep, sleeps 2 rounds after the last champion leaves range (results/arena-1020.md arenas 1-2; FACTS). Two Monoliths, (17,31) and (32,18), each in a walled pocket whose exit takes up to 5 steps (BFS on contest's walls).
- Damage per hit to a geared tank: 200 × 0.7 (Jungle emblem) × 0.9 (Durability emblem) = 126 (Pantheon's tanks at r214 take exactly 126).
- Objective: maximise tributed Monolith gems per game minus the gem value of our deaths (200 per carrier death; the 60-round respawn of others).

Decision rules (one champion, each turn, in `Plan::pre` before v4's health retreat):
1. **Target**: the Monolith alive by arrival with the lowest score 30·(h_M/20,000) + d(spawn, M) − 10·(allies claiming it) − 15·[current]; sticky until it is respawning.
2. **Gear gate** (P_S3_GEAR): a melee champion farms (v4) until it owns a Jungle and a Durability emblem of health, or until round 250. Contest buy list for melee: 5 Jungle shards of health, 5 Durability shards of health, then Power shards of health (30 gold each).
3. **Stage**: until min(P_S3_PACK = 2, alive) healthy allies are within 10 of M (sticky once met; cleared after 3 turns alone), wait at distance 6-8, outside range 5. While M is awake, walk in only behind a healthy ally already at distance 1 (that ally stays the nearest).
4. **Bands**: melee at distance 1, ranged at 2-3 (never the nearest while a tank stands at 1). The first hit waits until min(2, melee alive) tanks stand at 1. A ranged champion either moves or attacks in a turn (GameRules l.50), so it attacks first when in range.
5. **Leave**: with an ally strictly nearer, or tied at distance 1 with another ally: leave at h < max(0.2·h_max, 350) by stepping back one tile and recalling in place (5 turns; only damage interrupts a recall, and the Monolith hits the nearest). As the nearest: leave at h < 200·E(p) + 150, where E(p) = BFS steps from p to distance ≥ 6, and walk out greedily on that field.
6. **Heal**: recall when out of range with no enemy within 8; walk home around the Monolith's range; re-enter at h ≥ 0.6·h_max (v4's spawn heal-wait releases at 0.9).
7. **Capture**: Monolith dead with the reward pending: the visible ally with the most health within 6 walks onto the spawn tile; the others guard within 2. The 200-gem bag goes through v4's `bank()`; allies with h ≥ 0.5·h_max walk within 2 of a carrier with ≥ 100 gems (shared bytes 6-7, as siege.cpp).
8. **Interdiction** (P_S3_INTERDICT, P_S3_LOCK): an enemy in view with `is_submitting_gems()` and ≥ 30 gems within 10 tiles: place an altar lock (one carried, 10 gold) if within 2 tiles of that altar, else cast a stun (cancels the channel) or damage ability on it, else attack, else walk to it.
9. Draft (P_S3_DRAFT 2): Heart of Ruin, Ren, Stellar, Ryth, Varg (Sponge's contest draft: four melee tanks and no Cassi & Issac, whose partner deaths doubled our death count in the siege).
