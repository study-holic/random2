## Lessons that hold across experiments
- Local opponents (starter-cpp, v2, locker, tung-clone, our own older versions) cause 0.4 to 0.9 of our deaths per game; ladder opponents cause 6 to 10. A survival change cannot be measured locally; the two-raider testbed (EXP-088) and unranked ladder match groups are the only places it shows (D-061).
- A change that wins the 72-cell selection set has failed the 54-cell held-out set six times (v4.12, shop-recall, field-shop, centre, opening assignment v1 and v2, rows sticky approach). Selection-set gains alone never ship (D-055).
- Reaction rules layered on the farming loop (threat layer, bank-first, leash, hostility gate, synchronized stack) either misfire on harmless enemies and cut pace, or freeze farming. Five designs failed on 14 Oct (EXP-071, 072, 077, 078, 086, 087).
- Default identity is fuel-fragile: adding a field to a hot struct or any ungated code changes per-turn fuel and flips a game on monolith (EXP-082, EXP-090). tools/identity_check.py (four maps, forced rebuild) is mandatory after every v5-base edit.
- Knob-level imitation of the leader (Power build alone, solo collectors alone) does not reproduce its rows pace (EXP-091, EXP-092); its system has to be built as one policy.
