5.2 Key architectural elements (final state)
Target scoring (squads.cpp::choose_target)

double score = d + P_WAIT_WEIGHT * wait - std::min(P_GEM_WEIGHT * g.gems, P_GEM_CAP);
if (g.gems == 0 && gold >= P_GOLD_ENOUGH) score += P_GOLD_ONLY_PENALTY;
if (!g.reachable) continue;   // v21
Reachability at init (bot.cpp): one distance_field BFS from the ally spawn over walls | holes; a group is reachable if any ring tile is reached; altar_walkable additionally excludes static_block and camp spawn tiles. This single check turned islands from 0–360 gems at round 600 into ~1000.

Pathing through allies (util.cpp::move_towards_closest): if the first BFS yields no step, a second BFS treats ally champion tiles as passable (they move too) while the chosen step still must be free.

Monolith play (monolith.cpp): 1L << 40 is 0 in wasm32, so pick_monolith had returned −1 since v12; replaced with long long. gate_mode = Monolith present ∧ no walkable altar neighbour → full-time poking from Chebyshev 4 with line of sight, no banking or escorting until gate_open() (Monolith group respawning), bags capped at P_GATE_BAG 60 so tributes are short enough to survive stuns.

Squad structure (squads.cpp::assign_squads): P_ONE_SQUAD collapses the roster into one squad on 5-pick maps with both sides ≥ 40, excluding mono-only and rows maps.

Retreat rule (bot.cpp): need = max(maxHP·threshold, incoming·1.5 + 0.05·maxHP) (was 4 / 0.1). Tank rotation at 50%.

Draft (draft.cpp): 3-pick → Tavi, Stellar, Ryth (scr_prefs applied to every size-3 map); 5-pick unchanged (Tavi, Stellar, Ryth, Varg, Ren).

Stun staggering (util.cpp, enemies.cpp): skip is_cc() abilities when target.buffs().has_stun().

5.3 Pivots (bug → approach change)
Ranking drop 317→456 was not code regression — the map pool had been replaced at 21:51 on 19 Oct; v19 had never seen 10 of the 21 maps. Testing moved entirely to the new pool.
Uncapped gem weighting (0.3/gem) sent level-1 teams across the map to 3-Elk stacks (collision: 0 gems). Fixed by capping the bonus at 12 tiles.
"Fuel exhaustion on islands" was actually unreachable targets — 38 of 46 groups need a teleport. The trace showed MV … NO every turn; the probe's BFS confirmed it. Reachability filter added; the earlier fuel hypothesis dropped.
One squad everywhere broke small/special maps (contest, collision, fragment, monolith) while fixing Elk maps → gated by map size and mode.
Elk-stack "gather before engage" and "retreat-twice-then-shelve" both scored worse — squad mates chose different groups and waited alone. Code retained behind P_STACK_GATHER 0.
pick_monolith never worked — discovered only because boss_battle traces printed MP-1 with a valid group; the 32-bit long overflow explained every prior "poke play is weak" observation.
boss_battle altar is enclosed — walls/holes on every neighbour except the Monolith tile; bots stood beside the Monolith with 1000+-gem bags forever. gate_mode plus bag cap.
16-game samples were unreliable — two "corridors fixes" (conditional retreat, ranged poke-from-4) looked good on 6 maps and scored 99–101/168. All decisions moved to the 168-game bench; games are deterministic but chaotic under code changes.
Cassi & Issac on 3-pick maps — linked death doubled every loss (fragment: 29 deaths). Removing them was the largest single gain after v21 (+6 on the bench alone).
Engine-bug/reverse-engineering requests were declined; all work stayed within documented mechanics.
5.4 Engine facts established (verified, not assumed)
Camps do not regenerate; hitting one wakes the group, each monster attacks only within its own range 3 and never chases; sleep after 2 damage-free rounds.
Tiebreak at round 2000: gems → gold earned → kills; side B wins full ties. Side A acts first within a round (symmetric brawls favour A).
Tribute = ⌈gems/10⌉ turns; damage does not interrupt, stun/death/altar lock do.
Every champion exhausts fuel on turns 1–2 (init BFS and group tables); turns resume, only time is lost.
5.5 Open items / next steps
Ladder validation of v22: collect match-group zips of actual losses; self-play has reached diminishing returns.
corridors (0–2 both sides): the 2×2 four-Elk block has no tile in reach of fewer than two Elks; needs a dedicated plan (end-pair clearing or spell-only poking), and transit along the camp row must wait for monsters to sleep.
hooks / gold_rush as side B: centre brawls lost to first-mover advantage; consider a delayed/side-aware opening.
monolith (00) map: ~70 gems behind v20; 392-gem Monolith corners now pokeable since the long fix — untested as a deliberate strategy.
Teleport crystals: islands +392 gems (14 one-hop groups, 4 one-hop altars), big_world +192; requires buy-at-spawn → walk → channel-3 → farm → recall loop.
Turn-1 fuel: precompute reachability/group tables in the draft turn (10× fuel) into the 1024-byte draft memory.
Combat micro: generic parameters (chase, flee, focus, engage-HP floor) are exhausted (all ≤ baseline); further gains require per-map replay study.
Rejected, do not retry without a new idea: six-body roster on 5-pick maps (49/84), Monster shards ×10 first, P_THREAT_LAYER 1, old retreat buffer, gather-before-engage.
