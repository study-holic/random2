## 1. The gate

- **Chance a coin-flip bot passes a map** (≥ 7/12 and ≥ 2/4 strong, cells treated as independent): **34.9 %**; 5.9 of 17 maps pass by chance. A true 60 % bot passes a map 61 % of the time and all 17 maps 0.025 % of the time; a 70 % bot passes 84 % per map and 17/17 only 5 %. "Target 17 of 17" is unattainable for any bot that is not dominant, so it will be relaxed by hand mid-run. Fix the fallback before the first game.
- **The replace rule (+2 panel wins)**: with two equal bots, P(diff ≥ 2) = **27.1 %** per map; P(diff ≥ 4) = 7.6 %. Picking the best of 3 null cards on one map promotes a null change **61 %** of the time (1 − 0.729³). The tie-break clause (same wins, fewer strong losses, faster win round) adds more false promotions. D-048 already recorded that one-game deterministic cells flip like noise.
- **Panel strength**: foundry-v16 is "level with v4.14" (retest.md), the clones sit on foundry-v14's chassis at 0.85 fidelity (tarot.md:78), and the live MicroStella bot sat at 1,445 and lost 0-5 to a 1,442 team. So 7/12 against this panel means roughly "beats a 1,450 field". It says little about 1,500 to 1,650 opponents.
- **Named changes**: (a) count only cells whose outcome changed; replace when flips to win minus flips to loss ≥ 2, with no new loss against the strong pair, **and** a positive summed submitted-gem margin over the 12 cells (a paired metric uses the gem scores that wins discard). (b) Treat the gate as a regression filter: a candidate ships when it passes this filter **and** has a rule or ladder mechanism behind it, not on panel wins alone. (c) Record both numbers per map.

## 2. Plan as a whole

1. **Creativity.** The plan reruns the cloud session's own pipeline (cards → builders → gate) at a scale (25 cards, 6 to 8 builds, two waves) that needs infrastructure this laptop lacks. It is procedurally safe and too speculative on throughput. *Bolder*: if ladder rating at the lock counts, upload the best existing bot now and use the live ladder (about 30 ranked games an hour against real 1,400 to 1,550 opponents) as the test bed for one or two per-map draft swaps, judged on rating and watched losses. *Safer*: run the 34-game starter check on v4.14 and v4.15, upload the better one by 22:15, then make one draft-only per-map change and one cheap lock behaviour, and upload once more at about 23:15.
2. **Restriction.** The framing leaves out, with no evidence given: (i) shipping v4.14 or v4.15 early as a floor; (ii) the local v4.15 and v418-* candidates, which are newer than v4.14 and are never mentioned; (iii) a draft-only path built on a map fingerprint inside v4.14's own draft code, with no v1 hooks needed; (iv) porting foundry-v16's existing peel and lock tactics (tarot.md:50, :54) instead of writing new ones; (v) using MicroStella's own five 19:02Z ladder losses as the first watch target. "6 to 8 cards in two waves" is the wrong shape for about 75 build minutes.
3. **Causation.** The plan's implicit chain is: new behaviour → more panel wins → more ladder wins → higher standing. The middle link (panel → ladder) is correlation only, and others.md:89 records that local wins against own and passive bots "failed to predict platform results". The final link depends on open decision 1, which is still unanswered at 21:47.
4. **Limitations.** The panel is near v4.14's level. There are 12 deterministic cells a map. Best-of-several selection per map produces a winner's curse (above). Local CLI 2.1.4 differs from the 2.2.5 used for every cloud number, and the ladder engine version is unverified, so cloud measurements (contest table, 29 games/min) may not carry over. New hooks add fuel (80,000 cap; each `std::endl` about 3,500). New code paths that fire rarely (locks, monolith) are where crashes hide. The 16 Oct map snapshot may predate the 11:40 to 11:53Z re-layouts of collision, corners, expanse, interlock, lanes and packs (meta §2 first-ranked times). If the snapshot was taken earlier, 6 of the 17 maps are the old layouts.
5. **Feasibility.** The rebuild costs, estimated: CLI parity check or upgrade 5 to 10 min; map files checked against the 18 Oct ids 5 to 10 min; game runner (mapgate) 15 to 25 min; panel (clone Tarot, build foundry-v16 and 4 clones on 2.1.4, with compile risk) 15 to 30 min; v1 hooks (params.h, mapid.h, plan.h, 17 map files; note `C:/cambc-solo/mapid (1).h` exists and may be the cloud's) 30 to 45 min; one timed game 5 min. **Total 75 to 125 min, which is 66 to 110 % of the 113 min to 23:40.** Compute is not the constraint: full gate 204 + held-out 170 + starter 34 = 408 games, which takes 1.6 min at 3 s a game and 15.7 min at 30 s a game, over 13 jobs. The cloud's 29 games/min on 2 cores is 4.1 core-seconds a game, which is plausible next to contest's 3 s for 2,000 rounds, but time it. **Cut**: wave 2, the 25-card expansion, the cross-domain and retest quotas, the v1 hook rebuild unless a chosen card needs it, the clones if they do not build in 10 min (keep x-v4-14 and x-foundry-v16 as the panel), and the release /consult unless answers come back inside 10 min.
6. **Falsification (fix before tests).** The plan as written fails if infrastructure is not running by 22:30: no game-tested build is then possible before 23:20. In that case ship the floor bot and stop building. Its premise (that more local behaviour beats shipping early) fails if decision 1 says ladder rating at the lock counts **and** the floor bot beats the live MicroStella bot: every minute of delay then costs rating, about 1 point a minute at a 4-1 rate.
