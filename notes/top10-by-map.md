# Top-10 drafts, tempo and champion excess by map (18 Oct 2026, 09:20Z-19:13Z)

Source: `intel/matches-2026-10-18.csv`, 9245 ranked games, 17 current map ids (script at bottom). Written 21:47 BST 18 Oct.

## Definitions
- **Maps**: keyed by map id (first 8 hex shown). Name = CSV `map_name`, 1:1 with id and identical to `intel/meta-2026-10-18.md` section 2. `mapid (1).h` has only the 13 older maps, so it is not used.
- **Top 10**: the 10 team ids with the highest pre-group rating at their own last game in the window (Intercontinental Fish 2098, Mite 2074, procless 2018, Nyxius 2003, new team(4) 1970, Univardisty of Shingtonwash 1962, Sneaky Bush Campers 1949, hello 1939, invisibleAgain 1930, Boost 1922). Using peak rating instead gives the same set for 10/10 teams. Their games are taken from both sides (a top-10 team may sit on side a or b); 515 top-10 team-games in total (23-40 per map, so every top-10 draft cell is below 30 games; read them as anecdotes). Selecting on final rating biases the top-10 excess upward (overall +2.6 pp over 515 team-games).
- **Draft strings** are alphabetical in the data, so pick order is not visible; a draft is the champion set. C&I = CassiIssac.
- **Expected score** = 1/(1+10^((opp-own)/400)) from the pre-group ratings in the CSV. **Excess** = actual minus expected, in percentage points. Draft rows: `n, win% (excess)`.
- **Champion excess (all teams)**: every team-side of every game (both sides, 18490 team-sides), not only top 10. z = sum(actual-expected) / sqrt(sum e(1-e)); the two sides of one game are treated as independent, which slightly overstates |z|. Ranked among champions with n >= 30 on that map; champions below 30 are listed in the last column.
- **Tempo**: games won by a top-10 team (each game counted once). Round 2000 = game ended at the round cap and the lead decided it; "1000 gems first" = finished before 2000 with winner score >= 1000. Winner score is submitted gems.
- † marks any figure from fewer than 30 games. Sample: the CSV holds only teams in the top-50 and 1,450-1,600 samples and their opponents, so "all teams" means all teams in these games.
