# Map economics, 21 current maps (engine 1.0.0)

Regenerated 20 Oct by tools/maps21/econ21.py from the official map files (data/maps21.json). Group gems, gold and respawn are the engine's own per-group figures (they include any per-group reward multiplier the map sets); HP and strength use engine-1.0 camp designs (gem and gold clusters 120 HP). Spawn B is the mirror of spawn A (approximate); side = Chebyshev distance to A minus to B (negative = nearer A). The previous 17-map tables (engine 0.0.3, site-doc rewards) are in notes/map-economics-engine0.md.

Gem rate = sum over gem-bearing groups of gems / (respawn + 5 capture rounds).
