## 5. Copyable behaviours, ranked (interpretation unless marked Data)
1. **Do not die: 17 deaths a game v our 33-45.** Data: gap 1.9x to 2.6x; gear points and fights follow it. Rule: death drops carried gems and costs respawn time (GameRules.md, Death, recall and recovery); recalls cost 5 ticks (3 with Home) and are cancelled by damage, so top-10 recall 62 times a game (ours 37-43). Copy: recall earlier, before the fight, not mid-fight.
2. **Open every fight with an ability (85% v 35-43%).** Data. Rule: one successful ability cast a turn, 8-turn cooldown per slot, damage scales with Focus (GameRules.md, Abilities). v3b raised our share 35% to 43%, still half.
3. **Shop earlier and more (3.8 v 1.7 gear points per champion at round 200; 9.9 v 4.4 at 400).** Data. Rule: shards cost 30 gold, five combine to an emblem, bought on spawn tiles or while dead. Recalls double as shopping trips. Gear points are the strongest fight predictor in `notes/matchups.md`.
4. **Tribute bigger bags (41 v 24-30 gems) at the same tribute count.** Data. Rule: tribute takes ceil(gems/10) turn ends; damage does not stop it but stun, death, or a new lock does. Bag size is the difference between us and them; do not stop at a fixed small bag.
5. **Draft Stellar and Ren (96% and 72% v our 0).** Data for usage and for Ren+Varg+Ryth beating Tavi sets (n=18-22†). We play Vendetta (3% of top-10 sides) and Tavi (-10.8 pt in 1.0.0, n=121). A counter-draft test belongs in mapgate before it drives a decision.
6. **Altar locks: 5 of 8 teams with 1.0.0 data buy 7-12 a game; ours 0.** Data for usage. Rule: lock costs 10 gold, blocks enemy tributes, three basic attacks remove it, cancels existing channels. Evidence of benefit is weak: lockers v non-lockers in top-10 games won 16 of 31† (52%), tributes 30.7 v 24.3. Nyx, the strongest 1.0.0 team, buys none. Cheap to try on Mite-style maps (elk_arena, corners).
7. **Protect our spawn.** Data: enemy champions spend 8.9 / 7.3 champion-rounds near our spawn in rounds 100-400 against 0.8 for top-10 teams, so we are being camped and they are not. Rule: respawn at spawn tiles and shopping happen there. Interpretation: this feeds deaths in item 1.
8. **Claim camps early (15 groups by round 300 v 10-11); Nyx claims 22.6.** Data. Rule: capturing a camp group gives gold and gems. With 120-HP gem clusters under 1.0.0 this is cheap.
9. **Not a differentiator (Data):** early kills (0.4 v 0.5-0.7), team spread (12.6 v 12.5-14.7), fights near the enemy spawn (2% v 1%). Do not copy early aggression or splitting.
10. **Boss_battle and contest are won on the tiebreak:** boss_battle 74 of 122 top-10 games end on lifetime gold, 22 on gems, 26 on 1,000 gems; contest 24 gold, 27 gems, 38 gems-win (rule: gems, then lifetime gold, then kills). Gold income matters more there than tributes.

## 6. Limits
- Per team 12-20 sides (all †); only the all-top-10 row passes 30. Rating adjustment uses the 06:20 ratings, not game-time ones.
- Our v3b rows are presumed by time (games created from 05:10Z); the platform records no bot version.
- Recall count is a heuristic (a move of 8+ tiles ending within 3 of spawn); lock use is read from consumable bit 2. Gear points use the 0.0.3 item table; 1.0.0 stat changes do not alter the shard count.
- Locks, tributes and gems are per game and mix map sizes (2,000-round maps raise counts); the map mix is similar between top-10 and ours.
