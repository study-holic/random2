## 4. Policies
A Economy: rolling-horizon team orienteering with camp respawn windows (not full VRP). Utility of champion i on objective g = p*gems + lambda(t)*p*gold - expected loss - contest cost, over travel+clear+capture+recovery time; lambda changes with need for gear or gold tiebreak (proposed score, calibrate). Assign objectives for next ~30-60 rounds; re-evaluate on claim, sighting, death, bank threshold, respawn window; commitment margin against oscillation. Camp states: alive, being cleared, capture pending, claimed, respawning, blocked, contested. Bank: V_bank(b) = b*P(survive travel+channel) - mu*T_bank - V_opportunity; tribute length max(1,ceil(b/10)); damage does not interrupt, stun/death/lock do. Initial bag bands (to test): 10-20 dangerous central route or enemy stunners nearby; 20-40 normal route with accessible altar; 40-70 safe remote route/expensive return; recompute immediately when enemy carrier near or capture pending. Do not hoard forever. Spawn control: defensive objective near own spawn; raise cost of shopping/camping/routing through region after sighting; regroup and emerge together. Offensive blockade only when denied income > blocker opportunity cost; auto-release when threatened (occupancy rule verified; benefit not).
